## Extracted parameter notes

Crew 2; two Klimov VK-2500 (TV3-117VMA) turboshafts at 2,400 hp each. Length 13.50 m, coaxial main rotor diameter 14.50 m, height 4.90 m. Empty mass 7,800 kg, normal takeoff 9,800 kg, maximum takeoff 10,400 kg, warload 2,000 kg.

Maximum speed 300 km/h, cruise speed 270 km/h, range 1,160 km, combat range 520 km, service ceiling 5,500 m and static ceiling 3,600 m. Armament includes one 30 mm 2A42 cannon with 460 rounds, four external stations and Vikhr/Igla missile or gun/machine-gun pod options.
