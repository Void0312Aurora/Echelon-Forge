## Extracted parameter notes

Crew: one. Powerplant: two izdeliye 117 turbofans, 14,500 kgf each in the cited table (15,500 kgf alternate value shown by the same page). Geometry: length 20.9 m, height 4.65 m, wingspan 14.0 m, wing area 90 m².

Mass and fuel: 17,500 kg empty, 26,510 kg at 63% fuel plus missiles, 30,610 kg at 100% fuel plus missiles, 35,480 kg maximum takeoff, 11,000 kg internal fuel, 10,000 kg warload.

Performance: 2,600 km/h at high altitude, 1,300 km/h at low altitude, 20,000 m service ceiling, 4,300–5,500 km range, and +10–11 g maximum overload. The source does not state a combat radius.

Mission system and armament: AESA/radar and electro-optical systems are described; one 30 mm GSh-301 cannon with 150 rounds; internal weapon bays and named air-to-air, anti-ship, guided-bomb and air-to-surface stores are listed.
