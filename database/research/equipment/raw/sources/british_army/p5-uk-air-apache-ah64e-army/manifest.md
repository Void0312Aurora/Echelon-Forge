## Extracted parameter notes

The UK Apache uses two General Electric T700-GE-701D turboshafts, reaches 330 km/h, has a stated maximum weight of 7,746 kg, a 14.63 m main rotor and 4.72 m height. It carries a 30 mm chain gun, 70 mm rockets and Hellfire missiles; the Longbow radar can detect/classify up to 256 targets, display 128 and prioritise 16. Optical/thermal sights and an integrated defensive-aid suite are also stated.
