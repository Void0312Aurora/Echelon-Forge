## Why this package matters

The Tiger HAD's maximum take-off weight of 6,600 kg is 300 kg above the general Tiger figure of 6,100 kg that other publishers give for the HAP and UHT marks, and the difference is attributable to the Enhanced MTR390 fit. This sheet is the manufacturer's statement for the HAD specifically and is the source of record for that row.

It is also the only package held here that gives a fuel capacity, a mission duration and an armed range for this variant.
