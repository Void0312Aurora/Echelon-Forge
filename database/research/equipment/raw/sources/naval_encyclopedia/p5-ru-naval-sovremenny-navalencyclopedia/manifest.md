## Use and estimate basis

The article cross-checks hull geometry and displacement, reports two 37,000 kW steam turbines, official speed below 33 kt with higher trial claims, a 3,920 nmi range at 18 kt, crew alternatives (296 standard and up to 358 wartime), 4,900 kW electrical generation, roll stabilization, emergency propulsion and the Soviet sensor/weapon suite. Its 156.5 m/17.2 m geometry and 6,500 t/7,904 t displacement wording are retained as cross-checks rather than blended with the Forecast International table.

The page does not publish homogeneous armour thickness. The catalog uses only its directly described control, backup-power and EW features, and labels any categorical survivability envelope as a simulation estimate.
