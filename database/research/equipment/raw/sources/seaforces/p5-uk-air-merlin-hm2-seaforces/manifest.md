Dimensions from the block headed `Specifications (Merlin HM1)`: fuselage length 19.53 m (64 ft 1 in); height 6.62 m (21 ft 9 in); main rotor diameter 18.59 m (61 ft 0 in); main rotor area 271.51 m².

## Why this package matters

The Merlin HM2 leaf recorded its powerplant as unresolved, because the page held for it states a GE CT7-8E while the United Kingdom fleet is associated with the Rolls-Royce Turbomeca RTM322, and no held package named the RTM322 for the HM2.

This package names the RTM322-01 for the Royal Navy Merlin and states its take-off power. That resolves the direction of the divergence: the general AW101 document's CT7-8E is the export powerplant option, and the RTM322 is the engine in British service. The divergence on the leaf is retained as a recorded conflict rather than deleted, and the RTM322 reading now has a locator.

The engine power of 1,566 kW per engine also supplies the first HM-specific power figure the leaf has, recorded as an HM1-heading reading on an HM2 page.
