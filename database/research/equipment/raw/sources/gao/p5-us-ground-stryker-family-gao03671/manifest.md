## Why this source matters

This package exists to bound the `0.5 in` hull-armour figure carried from the Tier C compilation. GAO describes protection as capability against defined threats across defined arcs. That is a different kind of claim from a maximum plate thickness, and the two must not be equated.

The correct use of this source is negative: it is evidence that a single maximum plate value cannot be converted into a runtime protection equivalent, an all-around armour value, or an RHAe figure. The leaf rows carrying the `0.5 in` figure already say this; this source is the documentary basis for saying it.
