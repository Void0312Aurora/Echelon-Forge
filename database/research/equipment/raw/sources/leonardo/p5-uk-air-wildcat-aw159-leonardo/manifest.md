## Extracted parameter notes

Maximum gross weight (MTOW) 6,050 kg (13,338 lb). Maximum density altitude 4,572 m (15,000 ft); maximum pressure altitude 3,657 m (12,000 ft). Hover in ground effect at ISA and maximum gross weight 2,267 m (7,440 ft); hover out of ground effect 1,307 m (4,290 ft). Maximum range at 5,000 ft, ISA, maximum gross weight, 518 km (280 nm). Maximum endurance under the same reference condition three hours. Powerplant two LHTEC CTS800-4N turboshafts with FADEC. Rotor diameter 12.80 m (42 ft 0 in). Maximum cruise speed at sea level and maximum continuous power 264 km/h (143 kt); rate of climb 10 m/s (2,000 ft/min).
