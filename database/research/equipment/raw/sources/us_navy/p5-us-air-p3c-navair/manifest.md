## Extracted parameter notes

Mission: land-based long-range anti-submarine warfare and anti-surface warfare patrol aircraft.

Propulsion: four Allison T-56-A-14 turboprop engines, 4,600 hp each.

Geometry and mass: length 116.7 ft (35.57 m), height 33.7 ft (10.27 m), wingspan 99.6 ft (30.38 m), maximum takeoff mass 139,760 lb (63,394 kg).

Performance: 411 kt maximum airspeed; 328 kt cruise; 28,300 ft (8,626 m) ceiling; 2,380 nmi radius, or 1,346 nmi for three hours on station at 1,500 ft.

Crew and mission equipment: three pilots, two naval flight officers, two flight engineers, three sensor operators and one in-flight technician; advanced acoustic sensors, magnetic anomaly detection and mixed internal/wing-pylon weapons carriage.

Armament: AGM-84 Harpoon, AGM-84K SLAM-ER, AGM-65F Maverick, Mk 46/50/54 torpedoes, rockets, mines and depth bombs.
