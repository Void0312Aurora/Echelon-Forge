## Extracted parameter notes

Mission: multi-role attack and fighter aircraft.

Configuration-specific service: C/D models entered operational service in September 1987; the C model is a single-seat configuration and is in post-production support/retirement transition in the cited page's context.

Propulsion and geometry (NAVAIR A-D common block): two F404-GE-402 enhanced-performance turbofan engines, 17,700 lbf static thrust each; length 56 ft (16.8 m), height 15 ft 4 in (4.6 m), wingspan 40 ft 5 in (13.5 m).

Mass and performance (NAVAIR A-D common block): maximum takeoff gross mass 51,900 lb (23,537 kg); Mach 1.7+; ceiling 50,000+ ft; combat range 1,089 nmi clean plus two AIM-9s; ferry range 1,546 nmi with two AIM-9s and three 330-gallon tanks.

Crew and armament: one for the A and C models; one M61A1/A2 20 mm cannon; AIM-9, AIM-7, AIM-120, Harpoon, HARM, SLAM, SLAM-ER, Maverick, JSOW, JDAM, general-purpose bombs, mines and rockets.
