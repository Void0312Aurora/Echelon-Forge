## Extracted parameter notes

Technical specifications, F-15EX Eagle II block: crew 1 to 2 (pilot and weapon systems officer); length 19.4 m (63.7 ft); wingspan 13.1 m (42.8 ft); height 5.7 m (18.7 ft); wing area 56.5 m² (608.2 sq ft); empty weight 16,103 kg (35,501 lb); maximum takeoff weight 36,741 kg (81,000 lb); powerplant two General Electric F110-GE-129 at 129 kN each; ejection seat McDonnell Douglas ACES II.

Performance block: maximum speed 2,655 km/h (Mach 2.65); operational range 3,889 km (2,417 mi); service ceiling 18,000 m (59,055 ft); climb rate 250.0 m/s.
