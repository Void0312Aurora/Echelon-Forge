## Extracted parameter notes

Length 47 m (154 ft 2 in); wingspan 50 m (164 ft 1 in); height 15 m (49 ft 3 in). Empty weight 100,000 kg (220,462 lb). Maximum takeoff weight 220,000 kg (485,017 lb). Powerplant four Shenyang WS-20 turbofans in the B configuration; four D-30KP-2 in the earlier configuration.

Payload: the maximum payload is commonly given as 66 tonnes for the Y-20A.

Operators: the People's Liberation Army Air Force, with 55 Y-20 transports, 30 YY-20A and 10 YY-20B recorded in service as of 2026.

## A powerplant split held open

The four Shenyang WS-20 turbofans belong to the Y-20B configuration. The earlier Y-20 and Y-20A airframes use the D-30KP-2, which the YY-20A package in this tree records for the tanker. The two engine fits are recorded separately and are not merged onto one row.

## Why this package was added

The official source held for this leaf states that variant-level dimensions, payload and performance are not officially disclosed, which is why the leaf previously carried a single row recording them as not disclosed. This package fills those rows from secondary sources at Tier C.
