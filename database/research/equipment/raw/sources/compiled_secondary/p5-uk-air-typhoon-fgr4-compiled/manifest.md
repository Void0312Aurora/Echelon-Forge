## An internal contradiction on this page

The FGR.4 performance block gives a maximum speed of Mach 2.5 and a ceiling of 16,765 m. The page's own headline summary for the type gives 2,495 km/h and 16,765 m as well, so the headline and the block agree with each other on this aircraft.

A separate performance block on the same page attributed to the FGR.4 in an earlier revision of this manifest gave Mach 2.0. That reading was not returned by the retrieval of 2026-09-19 and is withdrawn. The Mach 2.0 figure that the Typhoon leaf previously carried therefore has no locator behind it in this tree, and the leaf no longer attributes it anywhere.

The leaf records the Royal Air Force figures for the FGR4 and does not adopt the Mach 2.5 reading, because the RAF technical block states Mach 1.6 and is the stronger source. The 2,495 km/h value is recorded here as an alternative rather than deleted.
