## Use

The handbook gives 45,086 lb empty, 57,544 lb combat, 65 km/h and 640 km maximum-cruising range, identifies the M993A1 Bradley carrier/M269 launcher-loader, two six-rocket or one-missile pods, GMLRS/ATACMS munition family and M270A2 upgrade boundary. The 640 km range is retained as an alternate to the earlier 483 km public figure.
