The operator data give the 500 hp diesel, automatic transmission, 64 km/h-class speed, 300 mi (483 km) range, 60% slope, 0.91 m wall and 1.02 m water-fording value. These are treated as a bounded carrier baseline and explicitly marked for A1 technical-manual cross-check.
