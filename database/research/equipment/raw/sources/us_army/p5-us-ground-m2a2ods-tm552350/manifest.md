## Use

Transport drawings and table provide 258 in (6.55 m) operating length, 131 in (3.33 m) operating width, 119.2 in (3.03 m) operating height, 117 in (2.97 m) reduced width, 103.9 in (2.64 m) reduced height, and the M2A2 67,282 lb operational-weight convention. The leaf keeps these transport semantics separate from the FM ODS combat-weight value.
