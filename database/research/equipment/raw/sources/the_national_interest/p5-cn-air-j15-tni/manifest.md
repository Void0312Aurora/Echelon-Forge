## Extracted parameter notes

Length 21.9 m; height 5.9 m; wingspan 14.7 m. Empty weight approximately 17,500 kg; maximum takeoff weight 33,000 kg. Powerplant two Shenyang WS-10 turbofans, 12,800 kgf thrust each in the article's units. Top speed Mach 2.4; range 3,500 km with external fuel tanks.

## Cross-check

A second specialist compilation gives the same three dimensions, an empty weight of 17,700 kg, a maximum takeoff weight of 32,500 to 33,000 kg, two WS-10H engines with Saturn AL-31F on early airframes, the same Mach 2.4 and the same 3,500 km range, and 12 hardpoints with a 30 mm cannon. The dimensions and speed agree; the empty weight differs by 200 kg between the two and both values are retained in the leaf.
