## Extracted parameter notes

Length 21.9 m; height 5.9 m; wingspan 14.7 m. Empty weight approximately 17,500 kg; maximum takeoff weight 33,000 kg. Powerplant two Shenyang WS-10 turbofans, 12,800 kgf thrust each in the article's units. Top speed Mach 2.4; range 3,500 km with external fuel tanks.

## Notes on this page's own content

The page gives the aircraft's dimensions, empty weight, maximum takeoff weight, engine and thrust, top speed, range with external tanks, and no hardpoint count.

Two claims on this page are contradicted or not corroborated elsewhere and must be read carefully. First, the range figure of 3,500 km is stated for external tanks, while `p5-cn-air-j15-deagel` places a 3,500 km reading on the leaf as a combat radius; the two are different quantities and the leaf records both readings. Second, the page does not state a seat configuration.

The 12-hardpoint count and the engine variant detail come from a separate Tier C database package, p5-cn-air-j15-deagel, not from this page.
