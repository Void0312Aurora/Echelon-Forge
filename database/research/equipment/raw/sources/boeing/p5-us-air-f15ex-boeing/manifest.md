## A note on the two payload figures

The specifications block gives 29,500 lb (13,381 kg) and the capability paragraph gives 29,500 lb (13,300 kg) for the same quantity. The imperial value is identical on both and only the metric rounding differs. The leaf records the imperial value with both metric roundings noted, rather than selecting one as the page's own conversion.
