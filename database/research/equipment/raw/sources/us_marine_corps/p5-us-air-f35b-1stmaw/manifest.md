## The 14,651 kg conversion is this package's own arithmetic

The page publishes the empty weight as 32,300 lb. It does not publish a metric equivalent for that row, although it does publish one for the maximum gross weight. The 14,651 kg figure used on the leaf is computed from 32,300 lb at 0.45359237 kg per pound, and the manifest states that it is a derived value rather than a figure the page prints. The leaf records the imperial value as the source value.

## Why this package matters

The maximum weight of 60,000 lb class on this page agrees with the prime contractor document held as `p5-us-air-f35b-lockheed`. An official service publication and the manufacturer therefore state the same class figure independently, which is the strongest provenance any mass row on the F-35B leaf now has.

It also supplies four rows the Royal Air Force aircraft page does not state at all: the empty weight of 32,300 lb, the internal fuel capacity band, the maximum g-rating of 7.0, and the two thrust figures.

The internal fuel band of 13,100 to 13,500 lb is wide enough to contain both the Royal Air Force comparison article's 13,100 lb (5,942 kg) and the prime contractor sheet's 13,500 lb (6,125 kg). It is recorded on the leaf as a band and the two narrower readings are recorded against their own packages rather than reconciled.
