## Extracted parameter notes

F-35B row: length 51.2 ft (15.6 m); wingspan 35 ft (10.7 m); wing area 460 ft² (42.7 m²); empty weight 32,300 lb (14,651 kg); maximum gross weight 60,000 lb class (27,216 kg); maximum speed Mach 1.6, approximately 1,200 mph; combat radius on internal fuel greater than 450 nautical miles (833 km); maximum range on internal fuel greater than 900 nautical miles (1,667 km).

## Why this package matters

The maximum weight of 60,000 lb class on this page agrees with the prime contractor document held as `p5-us-air-f35b-lockheed`. An official service publication and the manufacturer therefore state the same class figure independently, which is the strongest provenance any row on the F-35B leaf now has.

It also supplies three rows the Royal Air Force page held for this leaf does not: the empty weight of 32,300 lb (14,651 kg), the 460 ft² wing area reading as a Marine Corps figure beside the RAF reading, and the 15.6 m length as a third reading beside the RAF's 15.5 m and BAE Systems' 15.6 m.

The interior fuel figure of 5,942 kg on the leaf is the Marine Corps internal fuel capacity converted, and the two range figures on the leaf are this page's combat radius and maximum range on internal fuel.
