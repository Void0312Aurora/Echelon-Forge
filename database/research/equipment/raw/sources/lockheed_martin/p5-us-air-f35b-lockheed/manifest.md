## Extracted parameter notes

Maximum weight, F-35B: 60,000 lb class.

For contrast, and not recorded on the F-35B leaf: the same sheet gives the F-35A maximum weight as 70,000 lb class and the F-35C as 70,000 lb class.

## Why this package exists

The F-35B leaf previously carried a maximum take-off weight of 60,000 lb, converted to 27,200 kg, from a Tier C compilation that named no single artifact for the figure. The prime contractor's own document states the value and labels it a class, which is the reading the leaf now carries; the Tier C reading is retained as a recorded alternative on `p5-uk-air-f35b-migflug` rather than deleted.
