## Evidence used

The release identifies the V9 as the latest F-16C/D radar, reports a 30% detection-range increase over V7/V8, four versus two tracked targets in Situation Awareness search-while-track, larger search volume and improved TWS performance, improved single-target track, two-foot SAR resolution, and an antenna-mounted IMU for dynamic tracking/auto-boresight.

## Estimation / uncertainty metadata

- `track`: the four-target Situation Awareness figure is a mode-specific limit; it is not substituted for the separate 10-target TWS handbook figure.
- `range`: the 30% relative improvement is retained alongside Northrop's 33% figure because the releases compare different baselines/measurement points; no absolute range is derived from either percentage.
- `status`: chronology supports fielded status, while current fleet-retirement or sustainment dates require a separate current USAF source.
