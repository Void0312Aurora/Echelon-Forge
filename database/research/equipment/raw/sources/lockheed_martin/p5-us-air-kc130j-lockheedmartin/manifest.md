## A conflict this package opens

An encyclopedic entry states the KC-130J's maximum takeoff weight as 175,000 lb, which is 11,000 lb above the manufacturer's figure for the same aircraft and above the 164,000 lb structural limit the Air Force and the Air National Guard both publish for the C-130J family.

The manufacturer's 164,000 lb is the source-of-record reading on the leaf and the 175,000 lb is retained as a recorded alternative rather than deleted, because the encyclopedic figure may refer to a different clearance condition and neither page states its basis.
