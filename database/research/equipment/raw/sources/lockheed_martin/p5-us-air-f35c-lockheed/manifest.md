## Scope of this package

This package carries the F-35C column of the Fast Facts sheet: length, height, wingspan, wing area, horizontal tail span, empty weight, internal fuel capacity and weapons payload.

## Correction history for this package

An earlier revision of this manifest stated in its uncertainty line that an encyclopedic source and a compiled secondary record reproduced the same figures, and carried a cross-check giving a C wingspan of 43 ft, a height of 14.7 ft, a wing area of 668 sq ft, an empty weight of 34,581 lb against this document's 34,800 lb, and internal fuel of 19,750 lb, together with a note on the C's approach speed. Neither the encyclopedic entry nor the compiled record was named and neither could be re-located, so both claims are withdrawn.

The 34,581 lb against 34,800 lb comparison was the one substantive content in that cross-check and it is withdrawn with it. The leaf now carries the Fast Facts empty weight of 34,800 lb as a single-source figure with its tier attached, and the 172 lb difference is no longer presented as a cross-checked range.
