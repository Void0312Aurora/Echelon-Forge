## Extracted parameter notes

The Mirage 2000-5F is the final French air-defence version of the Mirage 2000, entered service in 1999. The cockpit uses five displays; the RDY radar replaces the RDI and supports multi-target operation; MICA missiles replace MAGIC and SUPER530 and provide Fox 3 capability.
