## Extracted parameter notes

Crew one or two, pilot and weapon systems officer. Length 63 ft 9.6 in (19.446 m); wingspan 42 ft 9.6 in (13.045 m); height 18 ft 6 in (5.64 m); wing area 608 sq ft (56.5 m²); airfoil root NACA 64A006.6 and tip NACA 64A203. Empty weight 31,700 lb (14,400 kg). Maximum takeoff weight 81,000 lb (37,000 kg). Powerplant two General Electric F110-GE-129 afterburning turbofans.
