## Extracted parameter notes

Specification block: crew 1 or 2 (pilot and weapon systems officer); length 63 ft 9.6 in (19.446 m); wingspan 42 ft 9.6 in (13.045 m); height 18 ft 6 in (5.64 m); wing area 608 sq ft (56.5 m²); airfoil root NACA 64A006.6, tip NACA 64A203; empty weight 31,700 lb (14,400 kg); maximum takeoff weight 81,000 lb (37,000 kg); powerplant two General Electric F110-GE-129 afterburning turbofans, 17,155 lbf (76.31 kN) dry and 29,500 lbf (131 kN) with afterburner.

Performance block: maximum speed 1,434 kn (2,656 km/h), Mach 2.5+ at high altitude; combat range 687 nmi (1,272 km); ferry range 2,600 nmi (4,800 km) with conformal and three external tanks; service ceiling 60,000 ft (18,000 m); g limits +9; rate of climb 50,000 ft/min (250 m/s); thrust-to-weight 0.93.

Armament: one 20 mm M61A1 Vulcan with 500 rounds; 23 hardpoints including four wing pylons; conformal tank racks carrying 29,500 lb (13,400 kg) of fuel and ordnance.

Production and units: ordered from 2020, with the order rising to 144 by May 2022 and a fiscal 2024 proposal for a planned fleet of 104. Squadrons listed include the 85th Test and Evaluation Squadron and 40th Flight Test Squadron at Eglin, Kadena with the 44th and 67th Fighter Squadrons, and the 107th Fighter Squadron at Selfridge as planned.

## Note on the shared specification block

The block on this page and the block posted on the F-16.net forum thread are the same text. The F-16.net post states its route to it explicitly: it reproduces the specifications section of the Wikipedia article on the Boeing F-15EX Eagle II, which in turn cites Air and Space Forces Magazine for the performance figures. Two locators therefore carry one upstream block and do not constitute two independent readings.
