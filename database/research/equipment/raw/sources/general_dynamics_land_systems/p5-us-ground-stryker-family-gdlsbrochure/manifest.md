Family survivability and mobility statements as published: high hard steel structure, add-on armor, SAF spall liner; 350 HP Caterpillar 3126 diesel engine; eight-wheel run-flat and central tire inflation system; 62.5 mph top speed (governed); 78 in trench crossing; 23 in vertical climb; 60 percent grade; 330 mi cruising range; 53 gal fuel capacity.

The brochure gives the M1132 Engineer Squad Vehicle a combat length of 298.5 in against a shipping length of 287.9 in. Those are two different configurations and must not be combined.

Note on variant naming: the brochure labels the mortar carrier simply "Mortar Carrier" and the NBC variant "NBC Reconnaissance". It does not distinguish the M1129 MC-A from MC-B sub-configurations, and it does not carry a separate M1252 MCVV entry.
