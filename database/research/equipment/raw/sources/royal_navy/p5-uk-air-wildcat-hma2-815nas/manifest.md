## Extracted parameter notes

The HMA2 delivers air-to-surface fires with Martlet, Sea Venom or an M3M heavy machine gun, air-to-air fires with Martlet and air-to-sub-surface fires with Sting Ray. It carries a sensor suite for extending ship situational awareness and operates from frigate and destroyer flight decks.
