## Extracted parameter notes

Headline statistics: lifting capacity 3.8 tonnes; range 750 nmi; speed 160 kt; length 23 m.

Merlin Mk2 role: principally anti-submarine warfare, with search and rescue, cargo transfer and maritime patrol roles; it can be role-fitted for airborne surveillance and control with an exterior-mounted radar.

Crew: four for anti-submarine operations — two pilots, one observer/mission commander responsible for navigation, weapons and radar, and one aircrewman covering the sonar. The airborne-surveillance role uses one pilot and two observers.
