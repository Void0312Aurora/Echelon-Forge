## Extracted parameter notes

Programme: also known as Zhi-20; first flight 23 December 2013; initial operational capability November 2018; origin China; corporations AVICOPTER and Harbin Aviation Industry.

Geometry: overall helicopter length 19.5 m; fuselage length 49.2 ft (15 m); width or span 52.5 ft (16 m); fuselage height 11.5 ft (3.5 m); overall height of the helicopter 5.0 m.

Masses and payload: empty weight 10,700 to 11,000 lb (4,850 to 5,000 kg); maximum takeoff weight 9,800 to 10,000 kg (20,000 to 22,000 lb); payload 1,500 kg (3,300 lb); weight external payload 5,000 kg.

Powerplant: 2 × WZ-10, Zhuzhou Aeroengine Factory; power 2 × 1,600 kW (2,145 hp); maximum power at takeoff 3,200 kW (4,290 hp). Main rotor blades 5; tail rotor blades 4.

Performance: maximum flight speed 320 km/h; cruising speed 170 mph (275 km/h, 150 kt); service ceiling 17,000 to 18,000 ft (5,400 to 5,500 m); maximum range 350 to 400 miles (560 to 600 km, 300 nm); maximum flight distance 1,200 km; ferry range 2,200 km; rate of climb 1,400 ft/min (425 m/min).

Roles listed: ground attack, close air support, anti-submarine warfare, anti-ship, transport, VIP transport, medical evacuation, search and rescue, reconnaissance.

## Two conflicts this table opens, both recorded

Overall height: this page gives 5.0 m; `p5-cn-air-z20-globalmilitary` and `p5-cn-air-z20-encyclopedic` both give 5.3 m. Both readings are on the leaf.

External payload: this page gives 5,000 kg; `p5-cn-air-z20-encyclopedic` gives 4,000 kg. Both readings are on the leaf, and the page's separate 1,500 kg payload row is the internal figure.

Maximum speed: this page gives 320 km/h for maximum flight speed while the same page's cruising speed is 275 km/h. The compilation and encyclopedic pages both give 360 km/h maximum and the encyclopedic page gives 290 km/h cruise. The pairs are internally consistent and disagree with each other by 40 km/h on the maximum, so the leaf records both.

## Correction history for this package

An earlier revision of this manifest recorded that this page stated the programme dates, the overall length and the rotor diameter and nothing else, and that a set of seven mass and performance figures had been attributed to it without being on it. The page in fact carries all of them, printed as bands, and the figures the earlier revision withdrew from the leaf are now restored from this page and from `p5-cn-air-z20-globalmilitary` and `p5-cn-air-z20-encyclopedic`.

The rotor diameter this page gives is a band, 16.2 to 16.5 m; the other two packages give a single 16 m. All are recorded.
