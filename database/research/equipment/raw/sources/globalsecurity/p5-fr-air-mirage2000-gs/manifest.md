## Extracted parameter notes

General characteristics: crew 1, or 2 for the B, D and N variants. Length 14.36 m (47.1 ft); length, two-seat, 14.55 m (47.7 ft); wingspan 9.13 m (30.0 ft); height 5.20 m (17.1 ft); wing area 41.0 m²; empty weight 7,500 kg (16,500 lb); maximum takeoff weight 17,000 kg (37,500 lb); internal fuel capacity 3,080 kg (6,790 lb); external stores capacity 6,300 kg (13,900 lb); hardpoints 9.

Powerplant: one SNECMA M53-P2 single-spool afterburning turbofan; thrust dry 54 kN (12,000 lbf); thrust with afterburner 95 kN (21,400 lbf); five high-pressure compressor stages; three low-pressure and two high-pressure turbine stages.

Performance, stated for the 2000C baseline with combat load: maximum speed Mach 2.2 (2,340 km/h) at altitude and 1,350 km/h at sea level; service ceiling 17,060 m (56,000 ft), or 18,290 m for the Dash-5; combat radius 1,550 km; ferry range 3,335 km; rate of climb at sea level 285 m/s; wing loading at combat weight 234 kg/m²; thrust-to-weight 0.91 at normal takeoff weight with afterburner; maximum g-loading +9.0 / −4.5; instantaneous turn rate 30 degrees per second.

## Applicability to the Mirage 2000D

The two-seat length of 14.55 m is the value applicable to the Mirage 2000D. The powerplant is the M53-P2, which is the engine fitted across the single-seat and two-seat Mirage 2000 family, and the page lists the B, D and N variants together as the two-seat airframes.

The performance block is written for the single-seat 2000C with combat load. The page states that two-seat and strike variants share fundamental performance characteristics, so the rows are recorded on the 2000D leaf as family performance values with the two-seat airframe noted, not as measured 2000D figures.

## Correction history for this package

An earlier revision of this manifest stated that an encyclopedic source gave the same M53-P2 engine, the same two thrust figures, and the same Mach 2.2, 1,550 km range, 17,060 m ceiling and 285 m/s climb rate, and treated that as corroboration of the performance block. The encyclopedic entry was never named and could not be re-located, so the corroboration claim is withdrawn. The performance block rests on this page alone, which is why every performance row on the 2000D leaf is labelled Tier C and carries the single-seat 2000C caveat.
