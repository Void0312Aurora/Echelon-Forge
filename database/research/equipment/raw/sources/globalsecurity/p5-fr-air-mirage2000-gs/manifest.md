## Extracted parameter notes

General characteristics: crew 1, or 2 for the B, D and N variants. Length 14.36 m (47.1 ft); length, two-seat, 14.55 m (47.7 ft); wingspan 9.13 m (30.0 ft); height 5.20 m (17.1 ft); wing area 41.0 m² (441 ft²); empty weight 7,500 kg (16,500 lb); maximum takeoff weight 17,000 kg (37,500 lb); internal fuel capacity 3,080 kg (6,790 lb); external stores capacity 6,300 kg (13,900 lb).

Powerplant: one SNECMA M53-P2 single-spool afterburning turbofan; thrust dry 54 kN (12,000 lbf); thrust with afterburner 95 kN (21,400 lbf); five high-pressure compressor stages; three low-pressure and two high-pressure turbine stages.

Performance, stated for the 2000C baseline with combat load: maximum speed Mach 2.2 (2,340 km/h) at altitude and 1,350 km/h at sea level; service ceiling 17,060 m (56,000 ft), or 18,290 m for the Dash-5; combat radius 1,550 km; ferry range 3,335 km; rate of climb at sea level 285 m/s; wing loading at combat weight 234 kg/m²; thrust-to-weight 0.91 at normal takeoff weight with afterburner; maximum g-loading +9.0 / −4.5; instantaneous turn rate 30 degrees per second.

Armament: two DEFA 554 30 mm revolver cannon with 125 rounds each, selectable 1,200 or 1,800 rounds per minute, muzzle velocity 815 m/s, effective range 1,000 m; nine hardpoints, five fuselage and four wing; Matra Magic 2, Matra Super 530D/F and MBDA MICA air-to-air missiles; AS-30L, ASMP and AM-39 Exocet; conventional and laser-guided bombs; 68 mm rocket pods; APACHE dispenser.

## Applicability limit

The two-seat length of 14.55 m is the value applicable to the Mirage 2000D. Everything in the performance and armament blocks is written for the single-seat 2000C interceptor and must not be recorded as a 2000D figure without a variant-level source.
