## What this package does and does not establish for the 2000D

It establishes that the 2000D is the two-seat fighter-bomber variant of the family, which the page states in prose, and it gives an independent reading of the M53-P2 powerplant for the family: 64.3 kN dry and 95.1 kN with afterburner.

It does not establish any 2000D measurement. Its table is a 2000C table. The performance, geometry and mass rows on the 2000D leaf that cite this package are recorded as 2000C readings on the single-seat baseline, and the leaf labels them that way rather than presenting them as family or 2000D values.

The dry-thrust split against the GlobalSecurity compilation, 54 kN against 64.3 kN for the same engine model, remains unresolved. Neither page states which rating it is describing, so no reading is preferred.

## Correction history for this package

An earlier revision of this manifest described the table as a single-seat 2000 baseline and presented its values as family readings. The page labels its own table as the 2000C, which is a narrower claim than the one the earlier revision made, and the description and the leaf rows were corrected accordingly.
