## Extracted parameter notes

Later J-11B and derivative engine fit: 2 × Shenyang WS-10A "Taihang" afterburning turbofans at approximately 132 kN (29,700 lbf) each with afterburner; the J-11BG uses the WS-10B at 13.2 tonnes of thrust.

Performance: maximum speed Mach 2.35 (approximately 2,500 km/h) at altitude; range 3,530 km (2,190 mi, 1,910 nmi); combat range approximately 1,500 km (930 mi, 810 nmi); service ceiling 19,000 m (62,000 ft); rate of climb 300 m/s; g limits +9 g.

## What this package supplies

It is the only package held for this family that states a speed, a range, a combat range, a ceiling, a climb rate and a g limit, so it is the source of record for those six rows. It states no dimension and no mass, so those rows rest on the Mitchell entry and the encyclopedic block.

Its engine thrust is a second reading of the WS-10A at the same 132 kN, which removes that row from single-source status.
