## Use

Government equipment data retained for the German PzH 2000 reference configuration. Public figures cover geometry, 57 t combat mass, MT881Ka-500/HSWL284C powerpack, road speed, range, crew, 60-round ammunition load and armament. Propellant-module and secondary-MG ammunition bounds in the leaf are explicit simulation estimates because the page does not enumerate those sub-loads.
