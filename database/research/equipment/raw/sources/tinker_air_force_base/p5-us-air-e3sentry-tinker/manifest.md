## Extracted parameter notes

Maximum takeoff weight 347,000 lb (156,150 kg). Optimum cruise speed 360 mph (Mach 0.48). Ceiling above 29,000 ft (8,788 m). Endurance more than eight hours unrefuelled. Unit cost 270 million USD in fiscal 1998 constant dollars. Crew flight crew of four plus a mission crew of 13 to 19 specialists, varying with mission. Date deployed March 1977. Inventory active force 33 including one test aircraft, reserve 0, guard 0.

## Conflict held open

The Air Force fact sheet states 325,000 lb (147,418 kg) maximum takeoff weight; this page states 347,000 lb (156,150 kg). Both are official Air Force publications. The 22,000 lb spread is not reconciled here, and the leaf records both rather than selecting one.
