## Conflicts against this source, left open

This page labels `287.9 in` as a length among its dimensions without stating a configuration. The GDLS manufacturer brochure gives the Engineer Squad Vehicle a combat length of 298.5 in and a shipping length of 287.9 in. The figure on this page therefore reproduces the shipping length, and the leaf treats it as such rather than as a combat value.

This page gives the crew as 8-10. The retained Army programme page gives 11 total. The leaf records both and treats neither as settled.
