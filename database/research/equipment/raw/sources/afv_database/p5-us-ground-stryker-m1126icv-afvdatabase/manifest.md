## Extracted parameter notes

Combat weight approximately 38,000 lb (17,200 kg). Height 122.88 in (312.12 cm); length 286.3 in (727.2 cm); width 116.43 in (295.73 cm); wheel clearance 21 in (53 cm). Maximum level road speed 60 mph (96 km/h); cruising range approximately 330 mi (530 km) on roads; minimum turning diameter 52 ft (16 m); maximum trench 78 in (200 cm); maximum vertical obstacle 23 in (58 cm); maximum fording depth 51 in (130 cm); maximum grade 60 percent; maximum sideslope 30 percent. Engine Caterpillar 3126, six-cylinder four-cycle inline turbocharged diesel, 350 hp at 2,500 rpm; fuel capacity 53 gal (200 L); transmission Allison MD 3066P, six forward and one reverse. Armor welded high-hard steel structure, maximum 0.5 in (1.3 cm). Armament .50 cal M2HB or 40 mm Mk 19 MOD3 on the M151E2 remote weapon system, 2,000 .50 cal rounds or 480 40 mm rounds, 60 deg/s traverse; an M240B 7.62 mm with 3,200 rounds was an adapter option. Crew 11 (commander, driver, nine passengers). First acceptance May 2002; manufacturer GM GDLS Defense Group, L.L.C.
