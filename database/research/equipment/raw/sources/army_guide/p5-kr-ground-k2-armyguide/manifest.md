## Use and estimate basis

Army Guide provides 10.8 m overall/7.5 m hull length, 3.6 m width, 2.4 m height, 55,000 kg early reference, 48 km/h off-road test value, 4.1 m snorkel crossing, 60-degree slope and 1.3 m obstacle bounds, 16-round autoloader plus 40-round total, KGPS/KCPS sights, mm-wave radar/MAWS, C4I/IFF/IVIS context and soft-kill warning/jamming. Secondary ammunition bounds in the leaf (300-500 x 12.7 mm and 2,000-3,000 x 7.62 mm) are simulation estimates based on comparable MBT stowage, not counted K2 inventory.

## Limitations

Tier C specialist compilation mixes prototype-era and production descriptions. Hyundai Rotem's current 56 t and 70/50 km/h values take precedence for baseline planning; Batch I-IV engine/transmission and export armor differences require separate cross-checks.
