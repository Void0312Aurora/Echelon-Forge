## Two conflicts this table opens, both recorded rather than resolved

Length: this table gives the B as 51.2 ft / 15.5 m. The same page's parenthetical converts 51.2 ft to 15.5 m although 51.2 ft is 15.6 m. The RAF aircraft page and BAE Systems and the prime contractor sheet all give 15.6 m. Both readings are retained on the leaf and neither is preferred.

Internal fuel: this table gives the B as 13,100 lb / 5,942 kg. The prime contractor sheet held as `p5-us-air-f35b-lockheed` gives 13,500 lb / 6,125 kg for the same mark, and a Tier C page gives approximately 13,500 lb. The United States Marine Corps page held as `p5-us-air-f35b-1stmaw` publishes a range of 13,100 to 13,500 lb (5,942 to 6,123 kg), which contains both readings. All three are recorded on the leaf.
