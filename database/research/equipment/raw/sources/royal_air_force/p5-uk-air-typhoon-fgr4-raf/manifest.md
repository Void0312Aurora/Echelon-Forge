## Extracted parameter notes

Technical specifications block: powerplant two Eurojet EJ200 turbojets; thrust 20,000 lb each; length 15.96 m; height 5.29 m; wingspan 11.09 m; wing area 50 m²; maximum speed Mach 1.6; maximum altitude 55,000 ft; air-to-air refuelling probe and drogue; aircrew one pilot.

Defensive aids: electronic counter and surveillance measures, missile approach warner, expendables, towed radar decoy.

Weapon systems: air-to-air Meteor, AMRAAM and ASRAAM; air-to-surface Paveway IV, Brimstone 2 and Storm Shadow; gun 27 mm Mauser.

Sensors: radar ECR 90; air-to-air targeting PIRATE infrared search and track; air-to-ground targeting and reconnaissance Litening V.

Role text: the F Mk2 was introduced for air-to-air combat and evolved into the FGR Mk4 with multi-role capability. Project Centurion transferred Storm Shadow, Brimstone and Meteor from Tornado GR4 before its March 2019 retirement. Combat debut 2011 in Operation Ellamy over Libya; Operation Shader from 2015. Six frontline squadrons, the Operational Conversion Unit, the joint UK-Qatari 12 Squadron, and 41 Test and Evaluation Squadron.
