## Extracted parameter notes

Two CFM56-7B jet engines; 27,000 lb thrust each; length 39.47 m; height 13.03 m; wingspan 37.64 m; maximum speed 490 kt; maximum altitude 41,000 ft; crew two pilots, one tactical coordinator and five weapon-system operators. Weapon systems include anti-shipping missiles and Mk54 torpedoes. Sensors include APY-10 radar, AN/AQQ-2(V)1 sonobuoy processor and MX-20 HD EO/IR; defensive aids include directed-infrared countermeasures, missile approach warning and countermeasure dispensers. The aircraft can carry up to 129 sonobuoys.
