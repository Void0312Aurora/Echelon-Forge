## Use and estimate basis

The brochure states family fording 1.5 m, 3+7 operators, 70 km/h, 900 km range, 16 t payload, Scania V8 engine and 25-120 mm turret options. Only the 1.5 m fording bound is carried into the Mk IV leaf, while 900 km and 16 t are explicitly rejected as older/light-family values.

## Limitations

The mirror is not an official BAE host and predates Mk IV. It cannot establish Mk IV customer sealing, armor, engine model or range.
