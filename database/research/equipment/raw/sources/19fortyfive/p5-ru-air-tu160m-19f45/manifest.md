## Extracted parameter notes

Powered by four Kuznetsov NK-32 afterburning turbofan engines. Maximum speed Mach 2.05 (2,220 km/h). Range 12,300 km (7,640 mi). Service ceiling 16,000 m (52,493 ft). Armament capacity up to 88,185 lb of ordnance, including nuclear and conventional bombs and cruise missiles. The modernization programme is described as ongoing, and a heavily upgraded Tu-160M performed a debut flight with new serially produced NK-32-02 engines per a United Aircraft Corporation press office statement.

## Cross-check and named corroboration

A second Tier C source gives length 54.1 m, empty weight 110,000 kg, titanium at 30 percent of the structure, a fly-by-wire control system, a blended wing profile, full-span leading-edge slats, double-slotted trailing-edge flaps, a cruciform tail, four NK-32 engines, Mach 2.05, and a four-man crew on K-36LM ejection seats.

The variable-geometry wing sweep, 35.6 m swept and 55.7 m unswept, and the height of 13.1 m are recorded from a third secondary source.
