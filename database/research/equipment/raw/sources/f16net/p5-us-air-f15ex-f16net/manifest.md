## Extracted parameter notes

Specification block as posted: crew 1 or 2; length 63 ft 9.6 in (19.446 m); wingspan 42 ft 9.6 in (13.045 m); height 18 ft 6 in (5.64 m); wing area 608 sq ft (56.5 m²); airfoil root NACA 64A006.6 and tip NACA 64A203; empty weight 31,700 lb (14,400 kg); maximum takeoff weight 81,000 lb (37,000 kg); powerplant two General Electric F110-GE-129 afterburning turbofans at 17,155 lbf (76.31 kN) dry and 29,500 lbf (131 kN) with afterburner.

Performance block as posted: maximum speed 1,434 kn (2,656 km/h), Mach 2.5+ at altitude; combat range 687 nmi (1,272 km); ferry range 2,600 nmi (4,800 km) with conformal and three external tanks; service ceiling 60,000 ft (18,000 m); g limits +9; rate of climb 50,000 ft/min (250 m/s).

## Relationship to the other F-15EX packages

The empty weight of 31,700 lb (14,400 kg) is the same value carried by the Lincs group page held as `p5-us-air-f15ex-lincs`. The two are the same block rather than two independent readings, and the post states the route by which it obtained it, so this package is a locator for the block and not a second measurement.

The dimensions and the 81,000 lb maximum takeoff weight agree with the Boeing product page held as `p5-us-air-f15ex-boeing`, which states them for the aircraft itself. Where the two differ, the manufacturer page is the source of record and this page is the recorded carrier of the compilation.
