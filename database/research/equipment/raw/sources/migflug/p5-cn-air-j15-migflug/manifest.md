## Extracted parameter notes

Identity and crew: baseline single-seat Shenyang J-15 for the PLA Naval Air Force; two-seat J-15S/J-15D derivatives are separate.

Geometry and mass: length 22.28 m; wingspan 14.70 m (7.40 m folded); height 5.92 m; empty mass 17,500 kg; normal takeoff mass 27,000 kg; maximum takeoff mass 33,000 kg; approximately 9,400 kg internal fuel; 6,500 kg external stores across twelve hardpoints.

Performance: maximum speed 2,300 km/h (Mach 2.17) at altitude; service ceiling 18,000 m; combat radius about 1,270 km under a light air-to-air profile; ferry range 3,500 km on internal fuel; the article identifies other Chinese-table values as disagreements rather than resolved facts.

Propulsion and systems: early baseline aircraft use two Saturn AL-31F afterburning turbofans at 125.5 kN reheat each; aircraft photographed from 2022 carry a reported navalised WS-10 family fit. Baseline radar is identified as Type 1493 pulse-Doppler; quadruplex digital fly-by-wire, arrestor hook, folding outer wings, strengthened landing gear, retractable refuelling probe, IRST, helmet-mounted sight and data link are described.

Weapons: 30 mm GSh-30-1 with 150 rounds; reported PL-8B/PL-10/PL-12 air-to-air, YJ-83K/YJ-83KH anti-ship, YJ-91 anti-radiation, KD-88, LS-6/LT-2 guided bombs, unguided bombs and rocket pods. The article says these are compiled from imagery and analysis, not an official clearance list.
