## Extracted parameter notes

H-6K: first flight 5 January 2007, in service October 2009, around 110 in service per the page. Two D-30KP-2 turbofans in enlarged wing-root intakes. Composite nose radome housing a large radar in place of the glazed navigator's station. Glass cockpit. Crew of four instead of six. No tail gun. Reinforced structure with composite panels. Six underwing pylons. Maximum weight increased from 79 to 95 tonnes. Combat radius roughly 3,500 km. Known in China as Zhanshen, God of War.

Baseline H-6 for contrast, not recorded in the leaf: length 34.80 m, height 10.36 m, wingspan 33.00 m, maximum takeoff weight 79,000 kg, two Xian WP8 engines.

## Cross-check

A second secondary source gives the H-6K maximum takeoff weight as 95,000 kg and the combat radius as approximately 3,500 km, agreeing with this page. It also gives the baseline H-6 length as 34.80 m, which is the value carried into the H-6K row with the caveat that the page does not state a length change for the rebuild.
