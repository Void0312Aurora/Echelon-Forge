## Extracted parameter notes

Empty weight 5,165 kg (11,387 lb), stated as published for the A and D, with the page's own note that the E runs several hundred kilogrammes heavier once the -701D engines, the uprated transmission and the Guardian avionics are fitted. Mission gross weight 8,006 kg (17,650 lb). Maximum take-off weight 10,433 kg (23,000 lb), with the British Apache AH.1 cleared to 9,525 kg (21,000 lb). Internal fuel approximately 1,420 litres (approximately 375 US gal, 312 imperial gal) in two crashworthy self-sealing cells fore and aft of the ammunition magazine.

## Why this package matters

It is the only package held here that states an internal fuel figure for this aircraft, and it is the only one that states plainly that the commonly published 5,165 kg empty weight belongs to the A and D marks rather than to the E.

Because it publishes no E empty weight, the leaf records the 5,165 kg as an A and D reading and states that no E figure is held. Filling that field with the A and D value unlabelled would have been the error this note exists to prevent.

The British AH.1 clearance of 9,525 kg is recorded as a separate mark's limit and is not carried onto the E leaf.
