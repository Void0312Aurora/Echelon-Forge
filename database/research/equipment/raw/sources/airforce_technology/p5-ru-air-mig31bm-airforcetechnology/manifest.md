## The length conflict this package opens

This page gives 21.5 m. An encyclopedic block and a defence magazine article both give 22.62 m for the same aircraft. The 1.12 m gap is too large to be a rounding difference. Both readings are recorded on the leaf and neither is preferred, because no package states which airframe configuration or whether the nose probe is included.

## What this package supplies

It is the source of record for the wing area, the loaded weight, the per-engine thrust in kilogrammes-force, the low-altitude speed, the ceiling, the range and the combat radius. Its maximum take-off weight is a second and third reading alongside the encyclopedic and magazine figures, which agree with it.
