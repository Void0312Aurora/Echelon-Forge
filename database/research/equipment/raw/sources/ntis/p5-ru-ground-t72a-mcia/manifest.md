## Use

The handbook gives a generic T-72 3-person crew, 125 mm gun, 60 km/h, 460 km range, 41,500 kg, 9.5 x 3.6 x 2.5 m and 1.2/5.0 m fording context. It is retained only as an independent historical cross-check; the T-72A leaf uses the Valka-specific values for declared parameters.

## Limitations

Generic T-72 values aggregate variants and should not be interpreted as a T-72A production-block specification without page-level reconciliation.
