## Extracted parameter notes

Four Pratt & Whitney F117-PW-100 turbofans at 40,440 lb thrust each. Dimensions: length 53 m, wingspan 51.75 m, height 16.79 m. Cargo compartment 26.82 m long, 5.48 m wide and 3.76 m high. Cruise speed Mach 0.74 at 28,000 ft; service ceiling 45,000 ft (13,716 m). Maximum takeoff weight 265,352 kg; maximum payload 77,519 kg. With 74,797 kg payload at an initial cruise altitude of 28,000 ft, unrefueled range is approximately 2,400 nmi. Crew is three (two pilots and one loadmaster); an aeromedical mission adds a basic five-person medical crew. Load options include 102 troops/paratroops, 36 litter and 54 ambulatory patients plus attendants, or 18 pallet positions.
