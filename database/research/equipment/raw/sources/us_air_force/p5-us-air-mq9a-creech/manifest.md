## Extracted parameter notes

Power plant Honeywell TPE331-10GD turboprop; 900 shaft horsepower maximum. Wingspan 66 ft (20.1 m); length 36 ft (11 m); height 12.5 ft (3.8 m). Weight 4,900 lb (2,223 kg) empty. Maximum takeoff weight 10,500 lb (4,760 kg). Fuel capacity 4,000 lb (602 gal). Payload 3,750 lb (1,701 kg). Cruise speed around 230 mph (200 kt). Range 1,150 mi (1,000 nm). Ceiling up to 50,000 ft (15,240 m). Armament combination of AGM-114 Hellfire, GBU-12 Paveway II and GBU-38 JDAM. Crew remote, two: pilot and sensor operator. Unit cost 56.5 million USD in fiscal 2011 dollars including four aircraft. Initial operating capability October 2007. Total force inventory 280.

## What this page adds to the main fact sheet

The ceiling of 50,000 ft is not published on the main Air Force MQ-9 fact sheet, which is why the leaf recorded that field as unestablished. This page supplies it.

This page also gives a cruise speed of around 230 mph, where the main fact sheet gives the range and payload figures but no speed at all. The two pages otherwise agree.

The sensor description differs between the versions: this 2020 sheet lists an image-intensified TV camera where the later main sheet lists a shortwave infrared camera. The leaf records the later main-sheet sensor fit and this page as the source of the ceiling.
