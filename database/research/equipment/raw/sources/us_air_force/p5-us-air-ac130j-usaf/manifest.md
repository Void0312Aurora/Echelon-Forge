## Extracted parameter notes

Mission: close air support, air interdiction and armed reconnaissance.

Propulsion: four Rolls-Royce AE 2100D3 turboprops; 4,700 shp each.

Geometry and performance: wingspan 39.7 m; length 29.3 m; height 11.9 m; maximum takeoff mass 164,000 lb; range 3,000 mi, limited by crew duty day with air refuelling.

Crew and armament: two pilots, two combat-systems officers and four special-mission aviators; 30 mm and 105 mm cannons plus GBU-39, GBU-69, AGM-114 and AGM-176 examples.

Mission systems: digital two-pilot flight station, mission-management console, communications suite, electro-optical/infrared sensors, advanced fire control and precision-guided munition delivery.
