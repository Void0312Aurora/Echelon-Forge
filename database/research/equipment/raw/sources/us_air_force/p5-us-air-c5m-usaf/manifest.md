## Extracted parameter notes

Mission: outsize cargo transport.

Propulsion: four General Electric F-138-GE100 engines (CF6-80C2-L1F family); 51,250 lbf each.

Geometry: wingspan 67.89 m; length 75.3 m; height 19.84 m; cargo compartment 43.8 m long, 5.79 m wide and 4.11 m high; 36 pallet positions.

Mass and payload: maximum cargo 127,460 kg; maximum takeoff mass 381,024 kg.

Performance and crew: 518 mph; about 4,800 nmi unrefuelled with 120,000 lb cargo, or about 7,000 nmi without cargo; crew is two pilots, two flight engineers and three loadmasters. Nose/aft doors and aerial-refuelling capability support the transport mission.
