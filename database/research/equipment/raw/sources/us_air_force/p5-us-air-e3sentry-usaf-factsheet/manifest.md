## Extracted parameter notes

Contractor Boeing Aerospace Co. Powerplant four Pratt and Whitney TF33-PW-100A turbofans, 20,500 lb thrust each at sea level. Rotodome 30 ft (9.1 m) diameter, 6 ft (1.8 m) thick, mounted 11 ft (3.33 m) above the fuselage. Wingspan 145 ft 9 in (44.4 m); length 152 ft 11 in (46.6 m); height 41 ft 9 in (13 m). Weight 205,000 lb (92,986 kg) zero fuel. Maximum takeoff weight 325,000 lb (147,418 kg). Optimum cruise speed 360 mph (Mach 0.48). Ceiling above 29,000 ft (8,788 m). Endurance more than eight hours unrefuelled. Crew flight crew of four plus a mission crew of 13 to 19 specialists. Date deployed March 1977.
