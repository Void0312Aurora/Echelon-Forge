## Extracted parameter notes

C-130J-30 column: length 112 ft 9 in (34.37 m as printed); wingspan 132 ft 7 in (40.41 m), identical to the standard C-130J; maximum take-off weight 164,000 lb (74,389 kg).

Standard C-130J column, recorded for contrast: length 97 ft 9 in (29.79 m); wingspan 132 ft 7 in (40.41 m); maximum take-off weight 164,000 lb (74,389 kg).

The table's own framing states the stretch is 15 ft (4.58 m) of additional fuselage and that the maximum take-off weight is an identical structural limit for both bodies rather than a J-30 reduction.

## Why this package matters

The C-130J-30 leaf previously recorded its maximum take-off weight as unrecorded, on the basis that the United States Air Force fact sheet held for it prints 164,000 lb for the C-130J and 155,000 lb for the C-130E/H with no J-30 column. This fact sheet, from an Air National Guard wing that flies the J-30, states the weight for the J-30 directly and explains that the limit is structural and common to both bodies. The leaf now records the value and cites this artifact.
