## Extracted parameter notes

Primary function suppression and destruction of enemy air defences, air and ground interdiction. Contractor Lockheed Martin Corporation. Power plant one Pratt and Whitney F100-PW-200/220/229 or one General Electric F110-GE-100/129. Thrust 29,000 lb. Wingspan 32 ft 8 in (9.8 m); length 49 ft 5 in (14.8 m); height 16 ft (4.8 m). Weight 19,700 lb without fuel (8,936 kg). Maximum takeoff weight 39,000 lb (17,690 kg). Speed 1,500 mph, Mach 2 at altitude. Range more than 2,002 mi ferry (1,740 nm). Ceiling above 50,000 ft (15 km). Armament one M-61A1 20 mm multibarrel cannon with 500 rounds, and external stations for up to six air-to-air missiles, targeting and visual acquisition pods, conventional munitions and electronic countermeasure pods. Crew one for the C. Initial operating capability Block 50/52, 1994. More than 800 F-16C produced. Cost new approximately 20 million USD, or 30 million in 2014 dollars.

The page also states the Block 50/52 carry the AGM-88 HARM with as many as four, and that the export configuration carries the APG-68(V9) radar.

## A maximum takeoff weight conflict held open

This page gives 39,000 lb. A separate specialist compilation gives 42,300 lb for the Block 50 and 37,500 lb for earlier C blocks. The 3,300 lb spread is larger than rounding and the two may describe different load or weight definitions; neither source states which. Both readings are recorded in the leaf and neither is preferred.
