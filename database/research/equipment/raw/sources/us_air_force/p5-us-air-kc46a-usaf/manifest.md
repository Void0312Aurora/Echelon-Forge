## Extracted parameter notes

Powerplant two Pratt & Whitney 4062 high-bypass turbofans, 62,000 lb thrust per engine. Speed 530 mph (850 km/h); service ceiling 40,100 ft (12,200 m); range 6,385 nmi (7,350 mi). Maximum cargo 65,000 lb (29,484 kg) or 58 passengers; 18 463L pallet positions; 15 permanent aircrew seats. The aircraft supports boom, centerline drogue and wing aerial-refueling pods, with the latter two systems independently operable as described by the source.
