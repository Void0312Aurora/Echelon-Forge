## Extracted parameter notes

Primary function close air support, airborne forward air control, combat search and rescue. Contractor Fairchild Republic Co. Power plant two General Electric TF34-GE-100 turbofans; thrust 9,065 lb each. Length 53 ft 4 in (16.16 m); height 14 ft 8 in (4.42 m); wingspan 57 ft 6 in (17.42 m). Weight 29,000 lb (13,154 kg). Maximum takeoff weight 51,000 lb (23,132 kg). Speed 420 mph / 365 kt (Mach 0.56) at altitude. Range 800 miles (695 nmi). Ceiling 45,000 ft (13,636 m). Payload up to 16,000 lb (7,200 kg) mixed ordnance. Crew one. A second Air Force source, the 127th Wing fact sheet, gives the same three dimensions.
