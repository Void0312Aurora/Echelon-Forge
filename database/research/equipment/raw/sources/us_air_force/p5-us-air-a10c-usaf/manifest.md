## Extracted parameter notes

Mission: close air support, airborne forward air control and combat search and rescue.

Propulsion: two General Electric TF34-GE-100 turbofans; 9,065 lbf thrust each.

Geometry and performance: length 16.16 m; height 4.42 m; wingspan 17.42 m; maximum speed 420 mph (Mach 0.56); ceiling 45,000 ft (13,636 m); range 800 mi (695 nmi); maximum takeoff mass 22,950 kg.

Crew and survivability: one pilot; titanium armour and protected self-sealing fuel cells are identified, but no armour thickness is published.

Armament: one 30 mm GAU-8/A; up to 16,000 lb (7,200 kg) mixed ordnance on eight under-wing and three under-fuselage stations, including AGM-65, guided and unguided bombs/rockets and AIM-9; countermeasure categories are also listed.
