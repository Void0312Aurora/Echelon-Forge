## Use and estimate basis

Direct page values are used for 13,000 kg (13,200 kg Sp3) mass, 6.735 x 2.940 x 2.068 m geometry, UTD-20 220 kW/295 hp, 5+1 synchromesh transmission, 65 km/h road, 40-45 km/h cross-country, 550-600 km road range, 3 crew + 8 troops, 40 x 73 mm rounds, 4 x 9M14M missiles, 2,000 PKT rounds, steel armor thicknesses, and amphibious 7 km/h-forward/2 km/h-reverse water performance. Standard 1PN22M2/PAZ/TDA system naming and the 460-500 km cross-country planning bound are retained as bounded configuration interpretations where the page does not expose every subseries detail.

## Limitations

Valka is a specialist community database and cites mixed historical references. Early short-bow and later long-bow hulls, smoke-launcher fit and export ammunition loads differ; BMP-1P and licensed BVP-1 are separate configurations.
