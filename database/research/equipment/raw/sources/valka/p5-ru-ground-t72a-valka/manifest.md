## Use and estimate basis

Direct page values are used for 41,500 kg combat mass, 9.53 m gun-forward length, 3.59 m width, 2.19 m height, V-46-6/573 kW engine, mechanical 7+1 transmission, 60 km/h road and 45 km/h cross-country speed, 460 km cross-country and 700 km road range, 3 crew, 44 main-gun rounds, 2,000 PKT rounds, 300 NSVT rounds, 2A46/2E28M armament, TPD-K1/TPN-3-49/TNPO/TKN sights, smoke/fire-protection systems, and 1.2/1.8/5.0 m fording bounds. Any 22-24 ready-round split, 0-1.3 km night sensor envelope, or 0-1.5 t refit mass delta is explicitly a simulation estimate, not a page measurement.

## Limitations

Valka is a specialist community database, not an official Soviet/Russian technical manual. Serial subseries differ in guns, engines, glacis and ERA; export T-72M/M1 and later T-72AB/B packages are outside this source boundary.
