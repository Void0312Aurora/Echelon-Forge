## Extracted parameter notes

The 18th and final modernized ATL2 was delivered to the French Navy in February 2026. The page gives approximately 37 m span, 32 m length, 11 m height, 46 t maximum takeoff mass, nearly 600 km/h maximum speed, 30,000 ft ceiling, 12 hours endurance and nearly 5,500 km range.

Standard 6 adds or updates the tactical mission system and sensor suite, including a Thales Search Master active-array radar, updated acoustic and sonobuoy processing, and new tactical display and mission consoles. The ATL2 remains a French maritime-patrol and armed anti-submarine platform.
