## Extracted parameter notes

The Eagle II is described as derived from the F-15QA ordered by Qatar and as the replacement for the F-15C/D in the United States Air Force, carrying the AMBER rack for up to 12 air-to-air missiles beside the F-15E-lineage heavy stores capability.

## Scope of this package

This package carries the programme-lineage statements only: the F-15QA derivation, the F-15C/D replacement role and the AMBER rack. It does not carry the F-15EX dimensions, masses, powerplant, speed or range, which rest on `p5-us-air-f15ex-lincs`, `p5-us-air-f15ex-f16net`, `p5-us-air-f15ex-globalmilitary` and `p5-us-air-f15ex-afsf`.
