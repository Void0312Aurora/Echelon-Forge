## Extracted parameter notes

Contractors Boeing, with BAE Systems for EPAWSS and Raytheon for AESA radar. First flight 2 February 2021. Delivered 11 March 2021 onward. Initial operational capability July 2024. Production 100 planned. Inventory 13. Operator ACC, AFMC and Air National Guard. Aircraft locations Eglin AFB, Portland Airport, with Fresno ANGB, Kadena AB, Klamath Falls, NAS JRB New Orleans and Selfridge ANGB planned.

## Cross-check on dimensions and mass

Two further secondary sources give the F-15EX dimensions and masses: length 63 ft 9.6 in or 19.4 m, wingspan 42 ft 9.6 in or 13.1 m, height 18 ft 6 in or 5.7 m, wing area 608 sq ft or 56.5 m², maximum takeoff weight 81,000 lb or 36,741 kg, and two General Electric F110-GE-129 engines at 129 kN each.

The empty weight differs between those two: 31,700 lb or 14,400 kg in one and 35,501 lb or 16,103 kg in the other. The 3,800 lb spread is larger than rounding and both readings are recorded in the leaf rather than reconciled.
