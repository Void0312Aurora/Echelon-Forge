## Use and estimate basis

Page values are used for 22.69 x 13.46 x 6.15 m geometry, 41,000 kg take-off mass, 3,000 km/h speed, 3,300 km range, two crew, Zaslon PESA, RK-RLDN/APD-518 datalinks, D-30F6 engines and R-33/GSh-6-23 weapons. D-30F6 afterburning thrust is represented as a 152-155 kN per-engine bound; the page's 800-round cannon figure is retained as an upper bound while 260 rounds is the common baseline estimate. Clean frontal RCS (15-25 m2) and 120-240 countermeasure cartridges are bounded specialist estimates.
