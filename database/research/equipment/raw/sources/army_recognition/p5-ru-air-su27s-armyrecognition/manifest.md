## Use and estimate basis

Page values are used for 21.9 x 14.7 x 5.92 m geometry, 30,450 kg take-off mass, 2,500 km/h speed, 3,530 km maximum range, one crew, N001 radar, OLS-27, GSh-30-1 and ten hardpoints. AL-31F afterburning thrust is represented as a 122-125 kN per-engine engineering bound from the named engine family; RCS and countermeasure quantities are specialist simulation estimates (10-15 m2 clean frontal RCS, 96-192 chaff/flare cartridges), not Russian service manual values.
