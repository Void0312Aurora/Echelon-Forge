## Use and estimate basis

Direct page values are used for 14,300 kg, 6.73 x 3.15 x 2.45 m, UTD-20 285/300 hp, 65 km/h, 7 km/h water speed, 550-600 km range, 3+7 crew/load, 500-round 30 mm load and 2,000-round PKT load. The page gives fire-control elevation/depression, rates of fire, night sights, NBC and smoke systems. A 4-missile ATGM ready-load is a bounded 2-4 estimate because the page names the launcher but does not state carried missile count; it is not a family-value substitution.
