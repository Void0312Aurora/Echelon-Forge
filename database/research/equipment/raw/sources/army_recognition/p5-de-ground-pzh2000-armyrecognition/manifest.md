## Use and estimate basis

Direct page values are used for 55,330 kg, 11.66 x 3.58 x 3.46 m, 1,000 hp MTU 881, Renk HSWL 284 automatic transmission (4 forward/2 reverse), 60 km/h, 420 km, 5 crew, 60-round shell magazine, 32-round primer magazine, 155 mm L/52 gun, 7.62 mm machine gun, fire-control computer, panoramic day/night sight, laser rangefinder, muzzle-velocity measurement and GPS/INS. The page does not state a modular-charge count; the leaf models 60 complete charge sets and a bounded 60-360 individual-module range (1-6 modules per shell) for simulation only. Secondary MG ammunition is a 500-2,000-round bound centered at 1,000 rounds, explicitly an estimate.
