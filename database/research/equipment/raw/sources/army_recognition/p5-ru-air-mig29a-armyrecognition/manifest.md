## Use and estimate basis

Page values are used for 17.37 x 11.4 x 4.73 m geometry, 18,500 kg take-off mass, two RD-33 engines, 2,445 km/h speed, 1,430 km maximum range, one crew, N019/RP-29 radar, OEPS-29 IRST/laser ranger and six hardpoints. RD-33 afterburning thrust is represented as the page's 81.4 kN maximum per engine; clean frontal RCS (3-5 m2) and 60-120 chaff/flare cartridges are bounded specialist estimates.
