## The BS conflation this page does not resolve

The page lists J-11BS as an active variant and gives one set of dimensions and masses for the whole family. It therefore does not support a claim that these figures are the twin-seat BS airframe's own; they are the family's. The 71.9 ft length in particular is the single-seat Flanker length, whereas the twin-seat airframes of this lineage are longer.

That distinction is recorded on the leaf rather than closed, because no package held here states a separate J-11BS dimension set.
