## Extracted parameter notes

Capacity, per mark: Il-76M 42,000 kg (93,000 lb); Il-76MD 48,000 kg (106,000 lb); Il-76MD-90A 60,000 kg (130,000 lb).

Dimensions: length 46.6 m (152 ft 11 in); wingspan 50.5 m (165 ft 8 in); height 14.42 m (47 ft 4 in); wing area 300 m² (3,200 sq ft).

Masses: empty weight 92,500 kg (203,928 lb), stated against the Il-76TD-90; maximum takeoff weight 210,000 kg (462,971 lb), stated for the Il-76MD-90A.

Powerplant: 4 × Aviadvigatel PS-90A-76 turbofans at 156.9 kN (35,300 lbf) each.

## What this package supplies

It is the source of record for the dimensions, and it is a second publisher for the 210 t maximum take-off weight, the 60 t payload and the PS-90A-76 engine. Its engine thrust of 156.9 kN per engine is the only per-engine thrust figure in this tree and is stated in SI rather than as the 16,000 kg the Russian Aviation table prints; the two are recorded as separate readings.

Its empty weight is labelled for the Il-76TD-90, so the leaf records it as a family reading and does not present it as the MD-90A's empty weight.
