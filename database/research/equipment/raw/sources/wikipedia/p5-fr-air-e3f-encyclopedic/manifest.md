## Extracted parameter notes

Variant line for this leaf: E-3F is the production aircraft for the French Air and Space Force.

Family specifications: crew, flight crew 4 and mission crew 13 to 19; length 152 ft 11 in (46.61 m); wingspan 145 ft 9 in (44.42 m); height 41 ft 4 in (12.60 m); wing area 3,050 sq ft (283 m²); empty weight 185,000 lb (83,915 kg); gross weight 344,000 lb (156,036 kg); maximum takeoff weight 347,000 lb (157,397 kg); powerplant 4 × Pratt and Whitney TF33-PW-100A turbofans at 21,500 lbf (96 kN) each.

## The powerplant question this package does not settle

A separate source states that the non-United States and non-NATO E-3 aircraft, which includes the French aircraft, are powered by four CFM-56-2 turbofans at about 24,000 lb thrust, while the United States and NATO aircraft use the TF33-PW-100A at about 21,000 lb. The block above gives the TF33 fit for the family.

No package held here states the E-3F's own engine fit or its own thrust, so the leaf records both the family TF33 reading and the CFM56-2 reading with their sources, and does not assert which is fitted to the French aircraft.

## The mass figures are family figures

The empty weight, gross weight and maximum takeoff weight above are stated for the E-3 family on the TF33 airframe. No package held here states fitted weights for the E-3F. The leaf records them as family readings and says so.
