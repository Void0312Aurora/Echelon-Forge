## Two readings this package contributes and one it does not

It contributes the overall length of 58 ft 2 in and 17.73 m, which is a different measurement from the 48.2 ft the manufacturer publishes and is recorded as such rather than as a competing value. It also contributes a third height reading, 3.87 m, which is well below both the manufacturer's 4.7 m and the Army Recognition 4.95 m; that spread is recorded on the leaf and not resolved, because the three pages do not state their measurement datum.

It does not contribute the powerplant. Its engine note names the -701 and the -701C and stops there, where the E mark is fitted with the -701D, so the powerplant row rests on the Army Recognition page.
