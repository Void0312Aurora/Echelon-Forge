## The crew field is the B, not the BS

The block states crew 1. The two-seat derivative is the J-11BS, and this leaf is named for the B and BS together. The block therefore describes the single-seat B airframe, and the leaf records it as such rather than presenting a single-seat crew figure as covering both marks.

No package held here states a separate J-11BS dimension, mass or crew figure. That gap is recorded on the leaf and not filled.
