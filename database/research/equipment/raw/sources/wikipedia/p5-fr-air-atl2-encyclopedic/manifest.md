## Relationship to the other ATL2 packages

Its wingspan, height and wing area are the metric readings the leaf carries, and the Military Factory page states the same three in imperial units. Its masses are identical to the Seaforces readings. Its powerplant is identical to the Seaforces reading.

It states no length, so the leaf's length row rests on the Military Factory page, and it states no performance figures, so the speed, ceiling and endurance rows rest on Seaforces.
