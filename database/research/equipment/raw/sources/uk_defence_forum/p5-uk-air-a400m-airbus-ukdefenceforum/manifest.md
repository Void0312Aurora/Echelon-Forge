## A weight conflict held open

This compilation states an empty or operating weight of 76,500 kg. Two other secondary sources state 78,600 kg. The 2,100 kg spread is larger than rounding, and the first figure is described here as an operating weight rather than a bare empty weight, which may explain it. Both readings are recorded in the leaf and neither is preferred.
