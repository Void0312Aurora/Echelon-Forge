## Relationship to the other Voyager packages

The Royal Air Force page states the KC2's distinguishing fit, two refuelling points against the KC3's additional centreline hose, and the cabin and cargo arrangement. It states no fuel quantity. This package supplies the fuel pair and is the source of record for those rows.

The 111,000 kg maximum agrees with the A330 MRTT type figure published by the manufacturer and held as `p5-eu-air-a330mrtt-airbus`, so the type-level number is not single-source. The 65,000 kg at 1,000 nautical miles offload figure is this package's alone.
