## Use and estimate basis

The page directly supports 156 m length, 18 m beam, 6.5 m draft, 7,500 t full-load displacement, 280 crew, 30 kt maximum speed, 4,500 nmi at 15 kt, one Z-9C/Ka-28 helicopter with hangar and flight deck, 64-cell VLS, Type 346A and supporting radar/sonar/EW systems, H/PJ-38 130 mm gun, H/PJ-12 30 mm CIWS, torpedo fit and CODOG propulsion. The page does not state normal/standard displacement, armour thickness, tested radar ranges or damage-control capacity. The catalog's 6,000-6,500 t standard-displacement band and categorical survivability envelope are explicitly bounded estimates, not official PLAN specifications.
