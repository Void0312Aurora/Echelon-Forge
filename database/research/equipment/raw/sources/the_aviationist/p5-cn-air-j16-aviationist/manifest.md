## Extracted parameter notes

Crew 2. Length 21.9 m (71 ft 10 in); wingspan 14.7 m (48 ft 3 in); height 6.36 m (20 ft 10 in). Empty weight 17,700 kg (39,000 lb). Maximum takeoff weight 35,000 kg (77,100 lb). Powerplant two Shenyang WS-10B afterburning turbofans, 135 kN (30,000 lbf) with afterburner. Maximum speed 2,120 km/h (Mach 2). Range 3,000 km (1,900 mi). Service ceiling 17,300 m (56,800 ft). Armament one 30 mm cannon and munitions on 12 external hardpoints, including air-to-air missiles, YJ-83 anti-ship missiles, AKF-98 air-to-surface missiles, KD-88 air-to-surface missiles and YJ-91.

Service scope: in service only with the PLAAF.

## Corrections applied to the leaf that cites this package

Two problems were found in the leaf, not in this page.

First, the leaf had recorded PLA Air Force and PLA Navy service. This page supports Air Force service only, and the official Chinese material held in the tree is Air Force material. The Navy claim was removed.

Second, the leaf had carried a ferry range of 4,000 km, a g limit of +9, a rate of climb of 230 m/s, and YJ-12 and YJ-83K in the weapon list, all attributed to this page. Those values are not on this page, which lists YJ-83, AKF-98, KD-88 and YJ-91 instead. Those claims came from sources that were never given a package, so the leaf now cites only what this page states and the unsupported claims are withdrawn.
