## Extracted parameter notes

Crew 2. Length 21.9 m (71 ft 10 in); wingspan 14.7 m (48 ft 3 in); height 6.36 m (20 ft 10 in). Empty weight 17,700 kg (39,000 lb). Maximum takeoff weight 35,000 kg (77,100 lb). Powerplant two Shenyang WS-10B afterburning turbofans, 135 kN (30,000 lbf) with afterburner. Maximum speed 2,120 km/h (Mach 2). Range 3,000 km.

## Cross-check and a conflict held open

A second secondary compilation gives length 72 ft, wingspan 48.2 ft, height 21 ft, empty weight 38,600 lb, maximum takeoff weight near 77,000 lb and two WS-10A turbofans at 33,000 lbf wet. Those agree with this page within rounding except for the engine sub-mark, WS-10B against WS-10A.

A third source states Mach 2.4, a maximum range of 1,620 nm, a combat range of 810 nm and a ceiling of 65,617 ft, which does not agree with the Mach 2 figure, the 3,000 km range or the ceiling of 17,300 m. The Mach 2 and 3,000 km pair is corroborated across two sources and is the value carried into the leaf; the Mach 2.4 reading is recorded as a conflicting claim rather than discarded.
