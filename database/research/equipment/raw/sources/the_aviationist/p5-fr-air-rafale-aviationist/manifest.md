## Extracted parameter notes

Crew one for the Rafale C and Rafale M, two for the Rafale B. Length 15.27 m (50 ft 1 in); wingspan 10.90 m (35 ft 9 in); height 5.34 m (17 ft 6 in). Empty weight 10,300 kg for the B, 9,850 kg (21,720 lb) for the C, and 10,600 kg (23,400 lb) for the M. Gross weight 15,000 kg (33,069 lb). Maximum takeoff weight 24,500 kg (54,013 lb). Fuel capacity 4,700 kg (10,362 lb) internal for the C and 4,400 kg (9,700 lb) for the B. Powerplant two Snecma M88-4e turbofans at 50.04 kN dry each. Armament one 30 mm GIAT 30 autocannon and up to 9,500 kg of external stores.

## Scope of this package

This package carries the variant table only: the per-mark crew, the dimensions, the per-mark empty weight, the gross weight, the maximum takeoff weight, the C and B internal fuel figures, the engine and the armament.

The empty weight is variant-specific and the page separates the three marks, which is why the C value is recorded on this leaf and the B and M values are not.

## Correction history for this package

An earlier revision of this manifest carried a cross-check stating that a museum entry gave the same wingspan, length, maximum takeoff weight and a 1,912 km/h maximum speed, and that a further compilation gave the same length, maximum takeoff weight and a 9,500 kg maximum external capability. Neither artifact was named and neither could be re-located, so the cross-check is withdrawn.

The leaf keeps the armament figure of up to 9,500 kg of external stores as this page's own statement rather than as an agreed value, and the 1,912 km/h maximum speed is withdrawn entirely because this page states no maximum speed.
