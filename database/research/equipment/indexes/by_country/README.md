## Responsibility

Country indexes provide retrieval paths into canonical equipment records. They do not own or duplicate equipment data.

Current country files include China, France, Germany, Russia, South Korea, Sweden, the United Kingdom, and the United States. This list is an index convenience, not an inventory statement.
