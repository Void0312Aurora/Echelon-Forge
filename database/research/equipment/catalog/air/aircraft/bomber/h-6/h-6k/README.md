## Configuration Boundary

The H-6K is a rebuild whose structure, engines, crew complement and maximum weight all differ from the baseline H-6. Maximum takeoff weight rose from 79 t to 95 t, crew fell from six to four, and the tail gun was deleted.

The three dimension rows are the baseline H-6 geometry. The cited source does not state that the rebuild changed the outer mould line, and it does not state that it did not either. They are therefore recorded as baseline values explicitly labelled, not as confirmed H-6K dimensions.

Every row except the armament row is Tier C secondary. The Chinese Ministry of National Defense material held here states the cruise-missile carriage and service presence but discloses no dimensions, mass or performance.
