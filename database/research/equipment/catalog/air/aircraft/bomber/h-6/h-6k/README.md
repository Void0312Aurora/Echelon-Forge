## Identity

- Family: H-6
- Variant: H-6K (Zhanshen)
- Role: Long-range strategic strike and anti-ship platform
- Manufacturer: Xi'an Aircraft Industrial Corporation
- Configuration scope: the H-6K is a major modernized H-6 variant and an extensively redesigned derivative of the airframe, changing engines, structure, crew size, nose configuration, systems and maximum weight. Only H-6K values are recorded.

## Configuration Boundary

The H-6K is a rebuild whose structure, engines, crew complement and maximum weight all differ from the baseline H-6. Maximum takeoff weight rose from 79 t to 95 t, crew fell from six to four, and the tail gun was deleted.

The dimension rows are now recorded as H-6K values rather than as labelled baseline values. The cited page states directly that the length of 34.80 m is unchanged from the Tu-16 despite the entirely new nose, and gives the wingspan and height as the H-6K figures. The earlier caveat, that the source neither affirmed nor denied a change to the outer mould line, no longer matches the page and has been withdrawn.

The same page carries an explicit caution of its own: China publishes no official performance data for any H-6 mark, a great many published H-6K weights are Tu-16 numbers carried across, and the page's own empty-weight row is flagged by it as the Tu-16 value verbatim and not credible for the H-6K. That caution is reproduced here, and no empty weight is recorded for this leaf.

Every row except the armament row is Tier C secondary. The Chinese Ministry of National Defense material held here states the cruise-missile carriage and service presence but discloses no dimensions, mass or performance.
