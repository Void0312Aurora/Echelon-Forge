## Configuration Boundary

NAVAIR publishes the geometry, propulsion, mass and performance values as an A-D Hornet common block. They are not relabelled as C-only certification values. The page explicitly assigns the one-seat crew to A/C models and identifies C/D operational entry in 1987. It does not publish C-only empty mass, fuel quantity or station-loading data; those fields are not inferred.
