## Configuration Boundary

Every parameter row on this leaf is Tier C secondary. The Chinese Ministry of National Defense release held here confirms service presence and discloses no dimensions, mass or performance, so no row can be raised above secondary confidence from the held material.

The empty weight and maximum takeoff weight each carry two readings from separate secondary compilations, differing by 200 kg and 500 kg respectively. Both are retained; the leaf does not average or select.

The J-15T is a catapult-capable variant that has been publicly displayed. It is not merged into this record and no figure is shared with it.
