## Configuration Boundary

The official source held here states the role and service presence, and explicitly does not disclose dimensions, performance or payload. Every other row is Tier C secondary and cannot be raised above that from the held material.

Two readings conflict and both are recorded. Maximum speed is Mach 2 in two sources and Mach 2.4 in a third. Service ceiling is 17,300 m in one source and 65,617 ft, which is about 20,000 m, in another. Neither conflict is resolved, and no row averages the pair.

The engine sub-mark also differs between sources, WS-10B against WS-10A; the WS-10B reading is carried with the alternative recorded in the source note.
