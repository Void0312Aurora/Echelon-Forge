## Configuration Boundary

The official source held here states the role and service presence and explicitly does not disclose dimensions, performance or payload. Every other row is Tier C secondary and cannot be raised above that from the held material.

Service scope is a corrected field. An earlier revision recorded PLA Air Force and PLA Navy service. The cited source states the type is in service only with the PLAAF, and the official Chinese material held here is Air Force material. PLA Navy service is not supported and has been removed.

Values withdrawn in this revision. An earlier revision carried a ferry range of 4,000 km, a g limit of +9, a rate of climb of 230 m/s, and YJ-12 and YJ-83K in the weapon list. Those claims were attributed to a second and a third source that were never given a source id or a URL, while the source column cited the primary article for every row. That produced citations that did not match the origin of the values, so the claims are withdrawn rather than left standing on an unnamed source. They can be restored when a named package is admitted for them.

The cruise-missile sub-types now recorded are the ones the cited source actually lists: YJ-83, AKF-98, KD-88 and YJ-91. YJ-12 and YJ-83K are not on that page.

The maximum speed reading of Mach 2 as it appears now is the value on the cited page. A Mach 2.4 reading from an unnamed source is withdrawn along with the other unnamed attributions.

The J-16 shares an airframe family with the J-11 and J-15 leaves. No dimension, mass or performance value is carried between them, because the three are different configurations.
