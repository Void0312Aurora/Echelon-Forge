## Configuration Boundary

The RAF aircraft page supplies powerplant, thrust and length and no wingspan, height, mass, speed or ceiling. Those five fields are filled from a Tier C compilation with a second Tier C corroborating source, and every row supplied that way is labelled C.

Dimensions carry two readings from the same compilation body: 11.0 m by 5.3 m in one version and 10.95 m by 5.28 m in another. The differences are rounding, and both are recorded rather than one being selected. A wargame wiki entry gives a length of 19.4 m, which conflicts with the 15.96 m the RAF page and the compilation both give, and is not adopted.

The FGR4 is the RAF designation for this variant. No value is carried from any other Typhoon leaf.
