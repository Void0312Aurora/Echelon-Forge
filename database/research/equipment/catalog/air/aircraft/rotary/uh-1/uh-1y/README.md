The NAVAIR crew line includes eight combat-equipped Marines and is retained as a mission complement rather than twelve cockpit crew. The Marine Corps payload and radius rows retain their HOGE and troop/fuel-reserve conditions. Fuel quantity, unrestricted payload and ferry range are not inferred. AH-1Z and other H-1 configurations are separate.
