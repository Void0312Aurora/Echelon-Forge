## Configuration Boundary

The cited source is a tag page for the E-3 Sentry rather than an E-3G-specific fact sheet. The airframe, powerplant and performance figures above therefore describe the E-3 family as that source publishes them, and they are not asserted as E-3G-unique values. The Block 40/45 mission-system modernization that distinguishes the E-3G is not quantified in this source: no radar range, tracking capacity, console count or datalink throughput figure is recorded.

Two variants of the maximum takeoff weight appear across Air Force pages: 325,000 lb on the fact sheet and 347,000 lb on the Tinker Air Force Base page. The fact-sheet value is recorded here and the divergence is left open rather than averaged.
