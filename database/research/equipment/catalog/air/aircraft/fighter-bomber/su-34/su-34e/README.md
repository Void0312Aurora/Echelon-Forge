## Configuration Boundary

The two official export sources use slightly different headline definitions: the ROSOBORONEXPORT sheet gives 45,100 kg maximum takeoff weight and 15,350 m ceiling, while the UAC export-analogue table gives 39,500 kg normal takeoff weight and 15 km ceiling. Both are retained with their labels; neither is silently substituted for the other. Empty mass, radar range and a station-by-station weapon table are not published in the held packages.
