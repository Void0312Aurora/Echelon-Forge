fix(database): retrieve and verify every source in the audit set, and record what retrieval returned

The audit of e92477de found that eight source packages created in that
commit carried extracted-parameter sections that had never been retrieved.
The values in them were written without any fetch. Every URL in the set was
retrieved on 2026-09-19 through the Tavily proxy and each manifest now
records what the retrieval actually returned.

Retrieved and confirmed as written
- p5-us-air-f35b-lockheed: the Fast Facts sheet returned its variant table,
  including the maximum weight row for the B at 60,000 lb class against
  70,000 lb class for A and C. The class qualifier is kept.
- p5-us-air-f15ex-lincs: returned the specification block, the performance
  block, the armament block and the production history.
- p5-us-air-f15ex-f16net: returned the specification post on thread page 1.
  The post states it reproduces the Wikipedia specification block, which
  cites Air and Space Forces Magazine. It is a third-hand carrier, and the
  manifest now says so.
- p5-us-air-f15ex-globalmilitary: returned the F-15EX version blocks. The
  page's operator table is wrong on this variant, crediting Saudi Arabia
  and Qatar with F-15EX aircraft that are F-15SA and F-15QA airframes, and
  is not admitted.
- p5-uk-air-typhoon-fgr4-encyclopedic: returned the general-characteristics
  block, including 10.95 m, 5.28 m, 51.2 m2, 11,000 kg, 16,000 kg,
  23,500 kg, the 4,500/7,600 kg fuel pair and the 60/90 kN thrust pair.
- p5-uk-air-f35b-migflug: returned the per-mark empty weight, internal fuel
  and maximum take-off weight rows.
- p5-us-air-f15ex-afsf: returned the technical data block, including first
  flight, delivery, IOC, production, inventory and operators.

Retrieved and found not to contain what was claimed
- p5-uk-air-typhoon-fgr4-forcesnews: two retrievals, at basic and advanced
  depth, returned the article's prose and no numeric specification table.
  The Mach 2.0, 90 kN, 15.96 m and 10.95 m values written into it are
  withdrawn; none is attributed to it, and the leaf no longer carries a
  Mach 2.0 maximum speed anywhere.
- p5-uk-air-f15ex-airvectors: the URL failed to fetch on three attempts and
  no working variant was located. The package is deleted and the programme
  lineage row it carried is removed rather than re-pointed.

Retrieved and added because they are stronger than what the audit found
- p5-us-air-f15ex-boeing: the manufacturer product page returned the
  dimensions, the 81,000 lb maximum takeoff weight, the 29,500 lb payload,
  Mach 2.5 and a 50,000 ft ceiling. Those rows move from Tier C to Tier B.
- p5-us-air-f35b-1stmaw: the 1st Marine Aircraft Wing aircraft facts table
  returned the F-35B row, including an empty weight of 32,300 lb (14,651
  kg) and the 60,000 lb class maximum gross weight with its own 27,216 kg
  conversion. The class figure now has an official service publisher to sit
  beside the prime contractor sheet, and the leaf gains its first empty
  weight for this mark.

Reader changes
- F-15EX: dimensions, maximum takeoff weight, payload, speed, ceiling and
  service life now rest on the manufacturer page. The three empty-weight
  rows are kept separate, with the note that two of them carry the same
  block rather than two measurements. The altitude conflict is recorded:
  the manufacturer gives 50,000 ft, the compilations give 60,000 ft.
- F-35B: gains a United States Marine Corps operator row, an empty weight,
  a maximum weight class row, and Marine Corps readings beside the Royal
  Air Force rows. One source id on the leaf was misspelled and is corrected.
- Typhoon FGR4: the maximum speed alternative is now 2,495 km/h from the
  GlobalMilitary FGR.4 block, which is the only package in the tree that
  states one. The general-type block's seat claim is withdrawn because the
  retrieval did not return a seat.

Three packages remain with their retrieval unconfirmed and say so in their
manifests: p5-uk-air-f35b-bae-compiled, whose JCR content path the proxy
did not return, and the two pre-existing packages p5-uk-air-f35b-raf and
p5-us-air-f15ex-usaf, which are single-artifact locators only.
