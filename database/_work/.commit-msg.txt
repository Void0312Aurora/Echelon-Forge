fix(database): make every source claim resolve to one named artifact

Audit of a56070d found four ways a claim could reach a leaf without a
traceable artifact, and one source-precedence regression. This commit
closes all five.

Source precedence (Typhoon FGR4)
- The RAF Typhoon page was previously treated as carrying powerplant,
  thrust and length only, on the strength of a truncated 2026-09-11
  capture. A re-read of 2026-09-18 returns the full technical block plus
  defensive aids, weapons and sensors. Those rows are now Tier A and the
  Tier C readings are retained as recorded alternatives rather than
  deleted: wingspan 11.09 vs 11.0/10.95, height 5.29 vs 5.3/5.28, wing
  area 50 vs 51.2, maximum speed Mach 1.6 vs Mach 2.0. The 19.4 m length
  reading is withdrawn because its artifact was never named.

C5 upgraded from keyword detection to source-artifact consistency
- 5a matches unnamed-source claims on word boundaries and a wider phrase
  set. The first version was bypassable by writing "two further secondary
  sources" instead of "a further source"; it now catches "further
  sources", "encyclopedic record", "compiled record", "three sources",
  "database entry" and the rest.
- 5b treats more than one URL line as an aggregate package. One source id
  must resolve to one locatable artifact; corroboration belongs in its own
  package with its own id.
- A correction-history sentence that quotes withdrawn vocabulary while
  also carrying withdrawal vocabulary is exempt, so the record of a
  withdrawal does not itself fail the gate. A sentence that merely
  mentions an earlier revision and then makes a live unnamed claim still
  fails.

Aggregate packages split or reduced to one artifact
- p5-uk-air-f35b-bae-compiled: second URL line removed; the maximum
  weight row moves to the prime contractor.
- p5-uk-air-typhoon-fgr4-compiled: second URL line removed; reduced to the
  GlobalMilitary FGR.4 block. Forces News and the general-type block move
  to p5-uk-air-typhoon-fgr4-forcesnews and
  p5-uk-air-typhoon-fgr4-encyclopedic.
- p5-us-ground-stryker-family-armor2002: Mirror line removed and recorded
  as a retrieval note in prose.
- p5-us-ground-stryker-family-gdlsbrochure: Alternate path line removed
  and recorded as a single-artifact note.
- p5-cn-air-y20-compiled: publisher and title no longer describe an
  aggregate of entries beside a single URL.

Unnamed secondary sources either named or withdrawn
- F-15EX: the dimensions, masses, powerplant, speed and range rows were
  attributed to a page that states none of them. They move to
  p5-us-air-f15ex-lincs (31,700 lb), p5-us-air-f15ex-f16net (same block),
  p5-us-air-f15ex-globalmilitary (35,501 lb, dimensions, MTOW, thrust)
  and p5-us-air-f15ex-airvectors (lineage). The empty weight is now three
  rows rather than one merged claim. Programme facts stay on
  p5-us-air-f15ex-afsf.
- F-35B: maximum weight now rests on the prime contractor document
  p5-us-air-f35b-lockheed and keeps the "60,000 lb class" qualifier
  rather than converting it to 27,200 kg. p5-uk-air-f35b-migflug holds the
  Tier C reading as the recorded alternative. Manufacturer dimensions are
  added from p5-uk-air-f35b-bae-compiled.
- Su-35S, Z-20, YY-20A, F/A-18E, Rafale C, Su-30SME, Mirage 2000D,
  F-35C and the Stryker M1128 gun row: the unnamed comparisons are
  withdrawn and the affected rows are removed rather than re-pointed. An
  unrecorded field is preferred to a value that no locatable artifact
  carries.

Rows removed with the claims that carried them: Z-20 height, empty
weight, maximum takeoff weight, external cargo, speed, range and ceiling;
YY-20A internal fuel, landing gear and the WS-20 contrast; the
encyclopedic F-35C empty-weight comparison; the Su-35S four-source
agreement sentence; the F/A-18E two-source corroboration sentence; the
