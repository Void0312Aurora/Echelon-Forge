### Archived F-16 FlightGear candidate

The previous root-level F-16 visualization asset is archived at
`examples/viz/web_viz/static/assets/archive/f16_flightgear_gplv2_candidate_20260611/f16.glb`.

asset. The current provenance review identifies it as a strong candidate
derivative of the FlightGear `NikolaiVChr/f16` GPLv2 aircraft package, so it
must not be treated as repository-level Apache-2.0 content.

Python packages declared in `pyproject.toml` also retain their upstream
